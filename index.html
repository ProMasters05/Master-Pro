Quick answers before you start.

Can I use Master Pro completely free?

Yes. The mastering controls, live preview, A/B comparison and presets built into the app are available to try free. The free plan includes 2 exports. Pro plans add unlimited exports, premium file settings and video export.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is needed to load, preview or master a track. Your audio is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to a mastering server.

Can I upload an MP4 or MOV?

Yes, in supported browsers. Master Pro extracts the soundtrack locally, then loads it into the mastering studio. Video codec support varies by browser; Chrome or Edge is recommended.

Will every track sound professional automatically?

Every mix is different. Start with Balanced or Streaming Safe, listen on headphones and speakers, and use the Original/Master buttons at matched volume. A loudness target is a guide, not a guarantee of platform approval or a substitute for a mastering engineer.
